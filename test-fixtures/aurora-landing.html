What's inside

Three small things,
done very well.

Instead of yet another productivity overhaul, Aurora replaces three habits you already have — with calmer versions that compound.

Focus mode

Pick one thing. Aurora quiets every notification, hides the dock, and dims windows you aren't using — until you say otherwise.

Calm rhythms

Pomodoros without the panic. Every cycle ends with a moment — a breath, a stretch, a glance out the window — not a klaxon.

Daily review

A two-line note at the end of the day — what landed, what didn't, what's next. So tomorrow starts already on the runway.
